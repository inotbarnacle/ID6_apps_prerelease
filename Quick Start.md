# Quick Start

1. Park the car and keep iDrive awake. Connect the computer directly over wired ENET.
2. Give your agent [the setup instructions](AgentReadMe.md), your choice of **MHD+ Suite**, **Bootmod3 Suite**, or **MG Flasher**, and whether you want xDrive Control (experimental).
3. Enable temporary SSH when needed. After the agent verifies the installed files, restart iDrive while parked and check My Vehicle.
4. To remove apps later, ask your agent to follow the [uninstall steps](AgentReadMe.md#uninstall).
